      High tight flags

      TickerRunFlag PullbackFlag vol Off 52w high
      A double or better inside 40 sessions, peaking within 5% of the 52-week high, then holding within 25% of that peak for 10–35 sessions. Read the count, not the names. This universe is large caps, so the number of flags is a clean gauge of speculative appetite while the names it returns are big companies that happen to fit the geometry.
      History
      Flag count against the share of S&P names above their 50-day. They are not the same signal: a slow, broad advance puts most names above the average with no flags at all, while a hot, narrow one produces flags on a middling breadth reading.
